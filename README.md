# IAMD Reimagined

Act as a Principal UI/UX Designer and Lead Web Developer. Rebuild the website for the Indian Association of Muscular Dystrophy (IAMD) currently at "https://iamd.in/" into a modern, accessible, high-performance web experience.

CRITICAL INSTRUCTION - IMAGES:

1. Retain and reference the exact image topics, photos, and historical assets from https://iamd.in/ (e.g., photos of IMDRC Solan center, hydrotherapy sessions, physiotherapy, awards photos with APJ Abdul Kalam/politicians, Boccia 2024 gold medalist Anjali Devi, and donor logos like Wagh Bakri). 

2. Display these images in crisp, modern containers with rounded corners (rounded-2xl), subtle inner drop shadows, hover elevation effects, and responsive picture tags.

DESIGN & COLOR PALETTE (MODERN & ACCESSIBLE):

- Theme: Professional, warm, highly compassionate, and accessible.

- Primary Colors: Deep Navy/Slate (#0F172A) for structure and high contrast text; Royal Trust Blue (#1D4ED8) for primary CTA buttons and links.

- Accent/Warmth Colors: Energetic Coral Orange (#F97316) for donation actions and highlight badges; Soft Sage/Emerald Green (#059669) for success stories and health indicators.

- Backgrounds: Clean warm off-white (#FAFAFA) and muted slate cards (#F1F5F9) to eliminate eye strain.

- Typography: Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans (ultra-legible, modern sans-serif with strong hierarchy and high readability for visually impaired users).

MODERN FEATURES & INTERACTIVE ELEMENTS TO INCLUDE:

1. Top Accessibility Toolbar:

   - Sticky floating or top header toolbar with toggles for: [High Contrast Mode], [Font Size + / -], and [Voice Reader / Screen Reader Ready].

2. Header & Global Navigation:

   - Clean, sticky frosted-glass header (backdrop-blur).

   - Emergency/Helpline banner at the top with clickable tel: links (01792 292037 / 92180 98999).

   - Clear nav links: About Us, Facilities & Booking, Muscular Dystrophy Care, Success Stories, Donate.

   - High-visibility CTA button: "Book a Visit" and "Donate (80G Tax Free)".

3. Hero Section:

   - Headline: "Empowering Lives Affected by Muscular Dystrophy Since 1992"

   - Subtitle: Integrated Rehabilitation, Hydrotherapy, and Holistic Support at IMDRC Solan, Himachal Pradesh.

   - Direct Action CTAs: Dual buttons [Book Patient Rehabilitation Visit] + [Sponsor a Patient].

   - Live Stat Counter Badge: "30+ Years of Service | 10,000+ Families Empowered | 100% Non-Profit".

4. Reorganized Service Grid (Interactive Cards):

   - Modular Bento-grid layout replacing long text lists.

   - Interactive modal cards for: Hydrotherapy Care, Physiotherapy, Counseling, Healthy Dining, Accommodations, Therapeutic Yoga, and Regular Medical Checkups.

5. Patient Journey & Facility Tour (IMDRC Solan):

   - Highlighting the Solan rehabilitation center with an interactive photo/video gallery showcasing the real facility, dormitory rooms, and hydrotherapy pool.

6. Impact & Success Stories:

   - Highlight recent news (e.g., Gold Medalist Anjali Devi at World Boccia 2024 trained at IMDRC).

7. Simplified 1-Click Donation & 80G Tax Exemption Module:

   - Quick-select donation chips (₹500, ₹2,000, ₹10,000, Custom) clearly indicating what each donation funds.

   - Instant receipt download preview for Indian 80G tax benefits.

TECHNICAL FORMAT:

Generate the output using clean HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript (or Next.js/React component architecture), completely responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports, fully compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
